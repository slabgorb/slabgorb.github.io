WELCOME.TXT // read me first

Hi, I'm Keith. I build software, and I build the teams that build software.

For twenty-five years I've shipped products, led engineering orgs, and built the systems other people rely on. These days I architect multi-agent AI — orchestration frameworks that let engineers move at a pace that used to be impossible. Before that I rebuilt broken engineering orgs, ran remote teams before it was cool, and carried products through acquisition.

I'm known for three things: rebuilding teams that were written off, shipping under pressure, and building systems that earn trust. I'm looking for my next hard problem — staff / principal engineering, engineering leadership, or applied-AI architecture.
